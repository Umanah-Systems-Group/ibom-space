About

Machines that keep satellites working

Ibom Space is the hardware company of Umanah Systems Group. We design everything in Easley, our own engineering platform, and simulate each mission there before anything is built. Agbára, the servicing tug, is the first machine; Igwe propulsion and Caelum communications are sold as parts.

Flown in Easley before it is built

Easley is our sister company’s mission engineering platform. Every Ibom part and every tug mission is designed and simulated there first, from the approach corridor to the push through the centre of mass.

With no hardware yet, that is where the work happens today: one living model of the spacecraft, every analysis run against it, and the evidence kept for the people who will buy it.

Where we are

Today Ibom Space is a design and a plan, with no flight hardware. This is the order in which we intend to prove it, each step with evidence a buyer can check.

  1. Early 2027Easley flies a complete tug mission: approach, capture, push and release, with every abort case passing.
  2. 2027An Igwe thruster fires on iodine on a test stand, and its results are checked against Easley.
  3. 2027The clamp grabs a test launch ring on an air-bearing table, including deliberate misses.
  4. ThenA flight demonstration: Agbára docks with a friendly target and pushes it under control.
  5. LaterMafuta: refuelling satellites that fly Igwe by swapping in a fresh solid-iodine cartridge, with no fluid ever transferred.
