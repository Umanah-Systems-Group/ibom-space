Splat vs mesh

A Gaussian splat is hundreds of thousands of soft, coloured blobs fitted to photos of the object, here the Blender renders. It replays the lighting it was trained on, so reflections are baked in and do not change as you move a light. The mesh model is real geometry with real materials: editable, measurable and relightable.
