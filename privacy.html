Privacy

Effective 9 November 2026

What this site collects

Nothing, unless you write to us. This website has no contact form, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Its fonts and scripts are served from this site, so your browser does not contact third parties to show it.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.

When you email us

If you email hello@ibomspace.com, we receive your name, address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of our conversation. We do not sell or rent it, and we do not share it for marketing.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep correspondence for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry. You can ask us to show you, correct or delete what we hold about you by emailing the same address.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date.
